Diamond Captain uses three diamonds and six thinking modes. Each diamond broadens the evidence or options ↗ and then focuses a decision ↘. This is the project's practical adaptation of Triple Diamond, not a universal definition of the method.

| Problem → | Solution → | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Discover ↗ → 2 Define ↘ | 3 Develop ↗ → 4 Deliver ↘ | 5 Deploy ↗ → 6 Digest ↘ |

**Loop:** return to the earliest decision changed by new evidence.

The arrows show typical handoffs. You can start in any phase, overlap work or return earlier when new evidence changes a decision.

| Phase | Main question | Useful output | What supports moving forward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Discover ↗ · Problem** | What is happening, for whom and why might it happen? | Relevant evidence, candidate problems and unknowns | Enough context to choose a problem; critical gaps are addressed or managed |
| **2 Define ↘ · Problem** | What is worth solving, and what change do we want? | Agreed problem, scope, goal and measurement plan | A justified proceed, reframe or stop decision |
| **3 Develop ↗ · Solution** | What different mechanisms could help? | Alternatives, small probes, trade-offs and risks | Enough evidence to select deliberately |
| **4 Deliver ↘ · Solution** | Which approach should we realize, and is it ready for use? | Verified implementation, service change or pilot, plus rollout and measurement plan | Relevant usability, feasibility, quality and launch requirements are handled |
| **5 Deploy ↗ · Outcome** | What happens in real use, including unexpected effects? | Usage, operations and feedback evidence with rollout context | An adequate observation window and usable data for the question |
| **6 Digest ↘ · Outcome** | What should we keep, change or investigate next? | Assessment, limitations, next decision and owner | Evidence supports a keep, improve, rollback, research or stop decision |

## What divergence means

In Discover, compare plausible causes before fixing on one explanation. In Develop, compare ways of helping: process, policy, content, service or interface changes. In Deploy, observe varied contexts and unexpected effects as well as the intended metric.

Research is useful in every phase. Qualitative and quantitative methods can support both exploration and selection. There is no required interview count, workshop or artifact list for every task.

## What convergence means

Make the next defensible decision using the evidence, trade-offs and limits. More research, a bounded pilot and a no-go are valid decisions. Convergence does not require pretending that every uncertainty has disappeared.

## What completion means

Completion follows the agreed task scope. A completed brief, a usable prototype, a released change and a reviewed live outcome are distinct results. Optional process activities remain optional; actual accessibility, privacy, safety and release obligations still apply.
