The agent executes the work with available evidence and authorized tools. You set the direction, constraints and decision authority. Product, engineering, research, operations and analytics can contribute throughout.

## Agree the result

At intake, establish the useful deliverable, scope, constraints, decision owner and where progress is saved. The agent should reuse supplied answers and ask one to three focused questions at a time, in your language.

Two terms help make the agreement concrete:

| Term | What it answers | Illustrative example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Design DoR — Definition of Ready** | What do we need to start useful work here? | An agreed problem, access to the current flow and permission to inspect redacted support cases |
| **Design DoD — Definition of Done** | What observable result will we accept? | A compared set of options with evidence, limitations and an accepted pilot recommendation |

These criteria support the decision. They do not turn optional activities into mandatory workshops or equate design acceptance with launch approval.

## Keep decisions separate

Readiness, the chosen phase, acceptance of a deliverable and permission to use a tool are separate facts. `READY` does not mean you accepted a result. Choosing Deploy does not prove a change is live. An unanswered question remains pending.

At a handoff, the agent asks whether to **accept, revise or reopen** the result, separately from choosing the next phase. It should not repeat a still-pending question or ask again where valid scoped delegation already settles the decision.

## Delegate routine choices if helpful

```text
Within the agreed scope, choose routine research and artifact formats.
Keep me involved when the problem, solution direction or release decision
changes. I will accept the final deliverable.
```

Delegation should state its scope. Silence and a preselected answer do not grant authority. Existing authorization remains valid; routine work should not require repeated permission.

## Expect an actionable handoff

A useful handoff gives the result and status, artifact links, actual checks and their limits, unresolved decisions with owners or proposed owners, and the next action. It ends with a copyable resume line. If live evidence is missing, an evidence-gap assessment can still be useful without claiming verified impact.

The skill cannot create tool access, run background work or guarantee memory across chats. External actions follow the host's permissions and your actual authorization.
