These are **illustrative scenarios and copyable starting prompts**. They are not reports of completed research, shipped changes or measured improvements.

## 1. Investigate mobile payment abandonment

**Starting point:** a symptom with uncertain causes; usually Discover.

```text
Use Diamond Captain. People abandon mobile payment more often than
desktop payment. Review the supplied funnel definitions and redacted
support cases. Compare plausible causes and data-quality gaps before
proposing a screen redesign. Deliver a focused problem statement and
the next investigation most likely to change our decision.
```

**Useful first result:** an evidence map, alternative explanations and one decision-focused investigation. Separate device differences, instrumentation gaps and observed user difficulty. Do not claim that the interface caused the difference from a funnel alone.

## 2. Reduce invoice approval delays without adding a UI

**Starting point:** Discover or Define, depending on existing evidence.

```text
Use Diamond Captain for invoice approval delays. We can change routing,
responsibilities, request content or policy; a new interface is optional.
Use the supplied process map and redacted cases to frame the bottleneck,
then compare materially different ways to help. Keep implementation
outside this first handoff.
```

**Useful result:** a supported brief and alternatives such as clearer intake, responsibility changes or routing rules, compared against the same goal. A service change is a valid deliverable. An untested proposal remains a proposal.

## 3. Validate concepts before a pilot

**Starting point:** Deliver when the brief and alternatives already exist.

```text
Use Diamond Captain in Deliver. The accepted brief and three concepts
are attached. Compare them against the agreed criteria, identify the
most consequential assumption, and prepare a bounded validation and
pilot plan. Ask me to choose the direction before implementation.
```

**Useful result:** a reasoned comparison, a focused validation protocol and a pilot recommendation. If no evaluation has run, report planned tests rather than invented findings. A pilot plan does not establish live exposure.

## 4. Review a shipped change with weak outcome evidence

**Starting point:** Deploy or Digest; evidence maturity may be insufficient.

```text
Use Diamond Captain to review our launched onboarding change.
We have ten days of usage and a few support messages. Check exposure,
metric definitions, baseline comparability and other changes in the
same period. Deliver what we can conclude, what we cannot conclude,
and a bounded next decision with an owner and review trigger.
```

**Useful result:** an honest outcome or evidence-gap assessment. If the observation window cannot answer the question, use `AWAITING LIVE EVIDENCE`. A positive before/after movement alone is not proof of causality.

## 5. Finish a small, reversible correction

**Starting point:** often Deliver, with phases compressed to fit the task.

```text
Use Diamond Captain for a small correction to this error message.
The reproduced issue, intended behavior and constraints are supplied.
Propose and verify a clear correction. Keep the work proportional to
the decision; flag any accessibility or behavior issue you discover.
```

**Useful result:** the corrected content and relevant verification, with its scope and acceptance clear. A small task does not require a full discovery program; applicable requirements still matter.
