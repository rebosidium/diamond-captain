# 8. Surveys and structured questionnaires

Choose when standardized answers are useful. A survey platform does not make a convenience sample representative or a leading question valid.

| Candidate | When to consider it | Route to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/) | Complex research or established experience programs | B |
| [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) | Conversational questionnaires with branching | B / A? |
| [SurveyMonkey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/) | General structured surveys and existing panels | B |
| [Google Forms](https://workspace.google.com/products/forms/) | Lightweight survey in a Google Workspace team | B / F |
| [Microsoft Forms](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/online-surveys-polls-quizzes) | Lightweight survey in a Microsoft workplace | B / F |
| [Tally](https://tally.so/) | Simple embedded or standalone forms | B |
| [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/) | Forms connected to an operational intake workflow | B |
| [LimeSurvey](https://www.limesurvey.org/) | Survey logic with a self-hosting option | B |
| [Alchemer](https://www.alchemer.com/) | Structured customer-feedback and survey workflows | B |
| [Survicate](https://survicate.com/) | Contextual website, product or customer surveys | B |

Use the [shared routing, capability limits and permissions](../tool-options.md#read-only-what-the-next-decision-needs) before acting.
