Choose by the decision and uncertainty in any phase; these are alternatives, not twenty required activities. Apply the shared [research guardrails](../research.md) and [UXR study, consent, permission and analysis guidance](../uxr.md). Prepare protocols when collection is unavailable; prepared materials are not findings. The linked sources support the methods; procedures below are concise adaptations.

## QL-01

**[Actual-event interview](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-in-depth-interviews)**

- **Prefer:** Explaining choices within a specific past episode.
- **Run:** Select a recent event; reconstruct actions, constraints and consequences; probe exceptions and workarounds.
- **Output:** Episode account with competing explanations.
- **Needs/limits:** Relevant lived experience; recollection and retrospective explanations can be incomplete.

## QL-02

**[Contextual observation and shadowing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/contextual-research-and-observation)**

- **Prefer:** Discovering interruptions, tools and handoffs absent from interviews.
- **Run:** Bound the activity; follow natural work; note actions and ask clarification at suitable pauses.
- **Output:** Situated workflow and workaround evidence.
- **Needs/limits:** Observable real activity; observer presence and interruptions may change behaviour.

## QL-03

**[Artifact elicitation](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/16094069261474629)**

- **Prefer:** Understanding how documents, messages or objects support work.
- **Run:** Invite a shareable artifact; ask its owner to explain creation, use and modifications; trace a concrete example.
- **Output:** Annotated artifact and practical requirements.
- **Needs/limits:** Owner interpretation; an artifact alone cannot establish intent or typical practice.

## QL-04

**[Diary study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/diary-studies/)**

- **Prefer:** Experiences dispersed across time or locations.
- **Run:** Define logging triggers; pilot a manageable entry format; review entries and clarify gaps afterward.
- **Output:** Contextual experience sequence and changes over time.
- **Needs/limits:** Sustainable participant effort; missing entries and reporting itself can distort the account.

## QL-05

**[Moderated qualitative usability](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing)**

- **Prefer:** Diagnosing difficulties while someone attempts realistic goals.
- **Run:** Define tasks and outcomes; observe without coaching; probe confusing moments after the attempt.
- **Output:** Task evidence, breakdowns and redesign questions.
- **Needs/limits:** Usable specimen and relevant users; distinguish assisted completion from independent completion.

## QL-06

**[Asynchronous qualitative usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/unmoderated-usability-testing/)**

- **Prefer:** Independent task attempts across schedules or locations.
- **Run:** Pilot self-contained tasks and stopping instructions; capture attempts and commentary; inspect each recording before coding.
- **Output:** Located task failures and explanatory comments.
- **Needs/limits:** Functional specimen and accessible capture; ambiguous behaviour cannot be probed immediately.

## QL-07

**[Concept comparison interview](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/which-ux-research-methods/)**

- **Prefer:** Comparing how alternative mechanisms might meet an established need.
- **Run:** Present comparably detailed concepts in varied order; ask for interpretation, use situations and trade-offs against current practice.
- **Output:** Mechanism assumptions and rejection conditions.
- **Needs/limits:** Understandable concepts; stated enthusiasm does not demonstrate demand or future use.

## QL-08

**[Service walkthrough](https://www.ida.liu.se/~matar63/PausingOrNot-short-Final.pdf)**

- **Prefer:** Examining transitions between digital, staff and offline interactions.
- **Run:** Select consecutive service moments; enact them using props and role holders; examine transitions and debrief.
- **Output:** Handoff failures and service-prototype changes.
- **Needs/limits:** Explicit roles and simulation boundaries; stand-ins and compressed timing limit realism.

## QL-09

**[Field follow-up](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/user-research-in-live)**

- **Prefer:** Learning what persisted, changed or failed after real use.
- **Run:** Revisit an exposed user/context; inspect a fresh task and adaptations; compare with earlier expectations and changed circumstances.
- **Output:** Adoption barriers and unintended effects.
- **Needs/limits:** Known exposure and elapsed use; attrition and other changes prevent causal attribution.

## QL-10

**[Laddering interview](https://doi.org/10.1080/00218499.1988.12467766)**

- **Prefer:** Understanding why a concrete product distinction matters personally.
- **Run:** Elicit a meaningful attribute; probe its consequences and importance; confirm the resulting chain in the participant's words.
- **Output:** Attribute–consequence–value links.
- **Needs/limits:** Skilled, non-pressuring probing; stop at uncertainty rather than inventing deeper motives.

## QL-11

**[Focus group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/focus-groups/)**

- **Prefer:** Exploring attitudes, vocabulary and socially negotiated disagreement.
- **Run:** Gather initial individual reactions; facilitate comparison around concrete stimuli; actively invite contrasting accounts.
- **Output:** Positions, shared language and contested assumptions.
- **Needs/limits:** Appropriate group composition; dominance and social pressure affect disclosures. Discussion cannot establish usability.

## QL-12

**[Open card sort](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/card-sorting-definition/)**

- **Prefer:** Discovering users' groupings before choosing an information structure.
- **Run:** Prepare representative content cards; invite grouping and naming; probe ambiguous placements and missing items.
- **Output:** Category rationales and candidate labels.
- **Needs/limits:** Understandable items; groupings suggest structures but do not demonstrate findability.

## QL-13

**[Qualitative tree test](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tree-testing/)**

- **Prefer:** Explaining why a proposed hierarchy helps or misleads.
- **Run:** Present a text hierarchy and realistic search goal; observe the path; probe label expectations after selection.
- **Output:** Misleading branches and label interpretations.
- **Needs/limits:** Defined hierarchy and destinations; absent visual context limits conclusions about the complete interface.

## QL-14

**[Cognitive mapping](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cognitive-mapping-user-research/)**

- **Prefer:** Exploring relationships within a complex domain or process.
- **Run:** Invite a participant-created map; ask about connections and boundaries; confirm the explanation while retaining contradictions.
- **Output:** Explained mental-model map and unresolved relationships.
- **Needs/limits:** Accessible mapping medium; facilitator prompts and drawing comfort influence the representation.

## QL-15

**[Content comprehension test](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2016/04/06/guest-post-looking-at-the-different-ways-to-test-content/)**

- **Prefer:** Checking whether instructions or messages support the intended decision.
- **Run:** Provide realistic content; ask users to explain meaning and their next action; trace interpretations to wording.
- **Output:** Misunderstandings and content revision targets.
- **Needs/limits:** Intended meanings and relevant readers; recognition or liking alone does not establish comprehension.

## QL-16

**[Desirability word elicitation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/microsoft-desirability-toolkit/)**

- **Prefer:** Exploring how a design communicates character or tone.
- **Run:** Expose the design; invite descriptive words using a balanced vocabulary; ask which elements prompted each association.
- **Output:** Attribute–element associations and unintended impressions.
- **Needs/limits:** Comparable stimuli; supplied words prime responses. Perceived trustworthiness is not verified trustworthiness.

## QL-17

**[Support-case analysis](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/user-research-in-live)**

- **Prefer:** Investigating existing accounts of service breakdowns.
- **Run:** Bound sources and dates; reconstruct distinct cases; code triggers, attempted remedies and unresolved outcomes; inspect counterexamples.
- **Output:** Breakdown taxonomy with case locators.
- **Needs/limits:** Usable case histories; reporting and support practices filter coverage. Ticket volume is not user prevalence.

## QL-18

**[Open-response qualitative survey](https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/output/6631905/the-online-survey-as-a-qualitative-research-tool)**

- **Prefer:** Independently written accounts without synchronous interviewing.
- **Run:** Pilot neutral open prompts; invite concrete experiences and an unanticipated-topic response; analyse accounts in context.
- **Output:** Varied experiences, language and hypotheses.
- **Needs/limits:** Accessible response format; writing burden, self-selection and unavailable follow-up shape depth and coverage.

## QL-19

**[Experience timeline reconstruction](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/researching-user-experiences)**

- **Prefer:** Recovering a journey that spans channels, waiting and repeated encounters.
- **Run:** Bound the journey; place events and actors along a timeline; probe gaps, transitions and reversals.
- **Output:** Participant-grounded journey with uncertainty marked.
- **Needs/limits:** Relevant experience; retrospective sequencing can omit events or compress time.

## QL-20

**[Collaborative-use study](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/sis_research/9658/)**

- **Prefer:** Understanding coordination when people perform a task together.
- **Run:** Give collaborators a shared goal; observe negotiation and control handovers; debrief differing accounts individually where useful.
- **Output:** Coordination breakdowns and shared-information needs.
- **Needs/limits:** Meaningful partner relationship and task; pairing changes behaviour and cannot represent independent use.
