# Methods by decision and phase

Use this menu when the next decision needs a concrete method. If an existing analysis or known procedure already answers it, continue without loading the catalog. The entries are options, not required activities or a maturity score.

## Open the relevant phase

Each phase menu offers 12 starting candidates, with conditions for choosing among them. A method may serve several phases; its procedure is described once in the shared library. Read the current phase menu and only the selected cards, not all six menus or all 60 methods.

| Thinking mode | Decision to inform | Menu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem / Discover ↗ | What is happening, for whom and under which conditions? | [Discover](discover.md) |
| Problem / Define ↘ | Which problem, scope and outcome are worth pursuing? | [Define](define.md) |
| Solution / Develop ↗ | Which materially different mechanisms deserve testing? | [Develop](develop.md) |
| Solution / Deliver ↘ | Is the selected solution usable, feasible and ready for its agreed handoff? | [Deliver](deliver.md) |
| Outcome / Deploy ↗ | What is actually happening during real use and operations? | [Deploy](deploy.md) |
| Outcome / Digest ↘ | What changed, how credible is that conclusion and what should happen next? | [Digest](digest.md) |

These six-mode routes are Diamond Captain's practical adaptation. Design Council's [framework](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/) describes four Double Diamond phases; [NN/g's research menu](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-research-cheat-sheet/) supports selecting methods throughout design. Neither source mandates this project's six-phase mapping or method counts.

## Shared method library

| Family | Contents | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Qualitative](qualitative.md) — QL-01…QL-20 | 20 observation, elicitation and qualitative evaluation procedures | Context, explanations, mental models, barriers and language |
| [Quantitative](quantitative.md) — QT-01…QT-20 | 20 measurement, analysis and study-design procedures | Magnitude, distributions, comparisons and appropriately designed effect estimates |
| [Design and delivery](design-and-delivery.md) — DS-01…DS-20 | 20 synthesis, framing, creation, evaluation and operational procedures | Turn evidence into alternatives, test assumptions and make a usable handoff or next-loop decision |

Families describe the main purpose, not an exclusive classification. An open-response survey can be analysed qualitatively; a diary can combine narrative with coded counts. The same activity does not become independent corroborating evidence because it appears under two labels. Qualitative/quantitative does not mean divergent/convergent or attitudinal/behavioural.

## Select and execute a method

1. Name the pending human decision and the uncertainty that could change it. Reuse credible existing sources and the active task checkpoint. An unsettled problem may need [alternative framing](design-and-delivery.md#ds-02) before evaluating a proposed solution; keep options and a recommendation separate from the human's choice. Preserve an already agreed framing and scope.
2. Choose a method whose inputs and access are available, or prepare its protocol if collection is not authorized. Pair methods only when they answer complementary uncertainties. No participant access means no claimed participant findings.
3. Adapt the small procedure on the card to the task. Follow shared [research guardrails](../research.md) and [UXR planning, consent and analysis](../uxr.md), including data quality, actual authority, privacy and sampling rationale. Advanced causal designs require their stated assumptions and competent analysis; descriptive findings are a valid result when those conditions fail.
4. Record method ID, question, source/sample/version, what actually happened, result, limitations and next decision in the existing journal or linked artifact. Label a proposed study, synthetic rehearsal, expert prediction and observed user evidence accurately. Preserve original evidence and superseded conclusions on a loop.

The method card's output is not automatic phase completion or user acceptance. Keep [phase readiness and handoffs](../phases.md) separate. Use [tools](../tool-options.md) or [office workflows](../office-tools.md) only after deciding what evidence/artifact the method needs; no named platform or new workshop is required.
