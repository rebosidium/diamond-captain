# Digest ↘ · Outcome

Judge the original goal and decide what to keep, improve, roll back, research or stop. Ask: what changed, for whom, at what cost, and which explanation is supported? A bounded conclusion of insufficient evidence is valid.

| Question or gap | Candidate | Prefer when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What changed across repeated experiences? | [QL-04 Diary study](qualitative.md#ql-04) | Longitudinal accounts can reveal variation or missing contexts. |
| Did the intended benefit persist? | [QL-09 Field follow-up](qualitative.md#ql-09) | Real use and changed circumstances can be revisited. |
| Which failures remain unresolved? | [QL-17 Support-case analysis](qualitative.md#ql-17) | Documented cases can challenge an aggregate success narrative. |
| Which sequence explains an outcome? | [QL-19 Experience timeline](qualitative.md#ql-19) | A cross-channel journey needs reconstruction; recall limits remain visible. |
| How much did the measured rate change? | [QT-02 Rate estimation](quantitative.md#qt-02) | Comparable eligible units, windows and missingness are understood. |
| What did the assigned experiment show? | [QT-14 Randomized A/B analysis](quantitative.md#qt-14) | A valid experiment was actually run; use its planned analysis and stopping rules. |
| Did comparable groups change differently? | [QT-16 Difference-in-differences](quantitative.md#qt-16) | A credible comparison and its causal assumptions are defensible. |
| Did the service trajectory change? | [QT-17 Controlled time series](quantitative.md#qt-17) | Repeated pre/post data and an unaffected comparison support specialist modelling. |
| Was the additional benefit worth the resources? | [QT-19 Cost-effectiveness](quantitative.md#qt-19) | Costs, comparable outcomes and credible incremental effects can be assessed. |
| Did the proposed mechanism match the outcome? | [DS-03 Outcome chain](design-and-delivery.md#ds-03) | Original assumptions and proxies need comparison with observed results. |
| Is continued operation sustainable? | [DS-13 Viability stress check](design-and-delivery.md#ds-13) | Actual demand, staffing and support costs challenge earlier estimates. |
| What should happen next? | [DS-20 Outcome review/loop](design-and-delivery.md#ds-20) | The decision must account for evidence, uncertainty, owners and a reopened phase. |

Prefer descriptive conclusions when the design cannot support attribution. Do not invent a past experiment or force a causal model onto inadequate data. Record contradictory evidence, alternative explanations, limitations and the next decision. On a loop preserve prior findings and superseded decisions, name the earliest affected phase and reopen its criteria; a new study is optional unless it can change the decision.

For prediction-versus-outcome learning, use [DS-20 calibration](design-and-delivery.md#ds-20) and retain the linked records in [product memory](../product-memory.md).
