# Harm beyond the immediate user

Read when framing a change, preparing a handoff or observing rollout if it can move costs, risk or control onto other people. Keep this proportional to the affected service and decision; record material risks in the existing task journal.

Completing a risk method does not complete a phase or establish the human's acceptance.
An available tool does not establish access or authority to act on other people or systems.
A proposed mitigation or predicted effect is not a verified safeguard or observed outcome.

## Inspect consequences and failure

| Review angle | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Affected parties | Include non-users, staff absorbing work, people represented in data and people unable to opt out; do not infer their agreement from a named role |
| Worst plausible circumstances | Walk the change as a user and as an operator under the most consequential plausible conditions; identify who bears the loss and who can recover |
| Incentives and patterns | Check dependence on confusion, manufactured urgency, default traps, asymmetric cancellation or unpaid effort transferred to staff |
| Vulnerability and variance | Consider low literacy, language mismatch, assistive technology, crisis context, unstable connectivity, shared/supervised devices and financial exposure where relevant |
| Scale and abuse | Examine repeated use, growth, deliberate misuse and unequal distribution of burdens; label predictions rather than inventing occurrence rates |
| Wrong data or unavailable automation | Define what happens when inputs are wrong, a dependency fails or automation is unavailable, including the human fallback and its real capacity |
| Detection and control | Name who could notice harm, receive a report, stop the change and restore a safe state; unassigned responsibility remains an explicit gap |

## Record the decision-relevant risks

| Risk | Affected party | Severity | Likelihood basis | Mitigation | Owner | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| State the harmful mechanism, not a generic warning | Name the role/group or mark unknown | Explain the consequence | Cite evidence or mark unknown; no invented probability | Mark proposed, implemented or verified with scope | Named or unassigned | Observable condition requiring intervention |

Preserve evidence, uncertainty and disagreement. Link a risk to the assumption or decision it affects; a low-confidence likelihood estimate does not make a serious consequence harmless. Check a proposed mitigation against the same failure conditions before calling it effective.

Unmitigated serious harm requires stopping the affected action and escalating to a named accountable owner; it is not a trade-off to weigh against velocity. If that owner is unassigned, record the blocker rather than inventing agreement. Continue only independent work that does not expose people to that harm.

This review supplements and never replaces legal, privacy, safety or accessibility obligations. A risk map does not authorize contacting affected parties, changing a live service or publishing personal information; follow [action permissions](dialogue-and-gates.md#dialogue-versus-tool-permission). The human owns product trade-offs within those obligations.
